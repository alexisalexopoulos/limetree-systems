Multi-Disciplinary Engineering

Our International Team Operates All Over the World

Established in 2025 in the Netherlands, Limetree Systems is a forward-looking company in the oil and gas and energy sector built on more than 30 years of industry expertise. With a highly experienced management team leading organizations such as Frames and Plug Power focused on the delivery of technically robust and commercially viable solutions.

  • Extensive Industry Expertise: Backed by over three decades of hands-on experience across oil & gas and clean energy domains.
  • Proven Leadership Track Record: Management team collaborated successfully at Frames and Plug Power, delivering complex, high-value projects
  • Integrated Project Delivery: At Frames, led the project on both sales and execution capacities, ensuring profitability and delivering operational excellence.
  • Advanced Technology Deployment: At Plug Power, developed the 5MW electrolyzer platform. Resulting with more than 50 units deployed globally across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

Bespoke Process Packages

From Concept Inception to Complete Packaged Solution Delivery

At Limetree Systems, we take full ownership of end-to-end engineering, procurement, and fabrication, minimizing complexity and actively mitigating project risk. Acting as a single point of accountability, we streamline interfaces and ensure full control over quality, cost, and schedule.

From early ideation and concept development through to final delivery and commissioning, we deliver fully customized, certified, and standards-compliant solutions. Each system is engineered for seamless integration, dependable performance, and precise alignment with your technical, operational, and commercial objectives, ensuring long-term reliability and value.

  • Execution Excellence: We deliver success through disciplined project management, rigorous quality control, and a relentless focus on meeting schedule, cost, and performance targets.
  • Integrated Value Chain: We align engineering, suppliers, and manufacturing into a single, coordinated workflow to ensure consistency, efficiency, and reliable outcomes.
  • Deep Technical Insight: Our team possesses a thorough understanding of the interfaces between design, procurement, and production, enabling informed decisions and risk mitigation at every stage.
  • Seamless Coordination: By actively managing dependencies and communication across all stakeholders, we ensure timely execution, reduced bottlenecks, and predictable project delivery.

Quality Assurance and Control at
Limetree Systems

Deliver packages at the highest quality standards and compliance.

1. What is Limetree Systems’ approach to quality?

Quality at Limetree Systems is a structural requirement, embedded in every phase of the project lifecycle: From engineering through to final delivery ensuring reliable and compliant outcomes.

2. Is Limetree Systems ISO certified?

Yes, Limetree Systems is ISO 9001 certified, confirming that its quality management system meets internationally recognized standards across all project activities.

3. How does Limetree Systems ensure consistent quality?

Through controlled processes, standardized workflows, and rigorous quality checks, ensuring repeatable, high-performance results for every project.

4. What level of compliance can clients expect?

Full compliance with applicable industry standards, client specifications, and regulatory requirements is ensured at every stage of execution.

5. How does quality management benefit my project?

It reduces risk, enhances reliability, and guarantees that deliverables meet performance expectations, on time and within specification.
